# Rescue sequence QA — 2026-10-06

The portrait cartoon presentation retains the existing courier identity, native menus, touch/mouse controls and safe-area fitting. The planning dock shows all five tools in the chain chapter, a live tool count, directional TURN and REMOVE controls, and optional goals. Turn/remove have 96 logical pixel touch heights (48 pixels at the tested 390×844 window). Placement ghosts and directional arrows show the intended intervention. A magnified inset tracks important impacts without changing scene or touch coordinates; the failure marker remains until rewind.

All eight authored solutions work. Wrong Address redirects a parcel onto a raised switch; Fragile Delivery catches the redirected parcel with a crate before it breaks the perch/bridge; Air Mail blows a falling crate onto a pressure plate; Express Route holds a gate with a crate and springs onto the moving platform before crossing the conveyor. Wrong Address also supports a shield-left + crate-on-switch solution. Broken versions of each chain fail, and nearby placements are tested.

See `docs/verification/test_results.json` and `visual_test_results.json` for assertions and outcomes. Rendered verification uses actual viewport pointer input for all four chains, plus turn/remove, failure, rewind, one-change repair, saved replay, results, pause/settings, and level select. Screenshots in `docs/screenshots` capture planning, impacts, results, the failed bridge chain, and saved replay.

The APK is a locally signed 0.3.0 debug build. Alignment, signatures, package/version, API levels, architecture and permissions are checked. No phone/emulator is available; on-device safe areas, touch, audio, vibration, battery and sustained performance remain unverified. Cloud llvmpipe frame rate is reported separately and is not an Android measurement. Player depth and difficulty have not been measured; use `docs/playtesting.md` next. Saved replays are local motion playback; there is no video/share export. Ropes and seesaws are deferred.
